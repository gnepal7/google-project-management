# Coaching an Agile team
  + Design the 'plays' with the team

# Coaching versus managing in Agile
  + Managing, it include:
    - Onboarding and orienting new employees
    - Conducting meetings
    - Delegating tasks and assignments
    - Monitoring progress and performance against those tasks
    - Making high-level decisions
  + Coaching: There are a few principles to keep in mind when coaching: 
    1. Motivate
    2. Support
    3. Encourage and appriciate
