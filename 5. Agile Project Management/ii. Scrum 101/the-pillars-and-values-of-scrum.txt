# The five values of Scrum
  1. Commitment
    - Personally committing to achieving the goals of teams or projects
  2. Courage
    - Team members to be couraged
  3. Focus
    - Necessary to focus on sprints / solutions
  4. Openness
    - Team members and stakeholders should be focus
  5. Respect
    - should respect the opinions, skills, and values
