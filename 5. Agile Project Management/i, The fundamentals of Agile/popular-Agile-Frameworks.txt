# Introduction to Scrum
  + Scrum
    . The word 'scrum' refers to the game rugby
  + Product Backlog
    - The central artifact in scrum, where all possible ideas, deliverables, 
      features, or tasks are captured for the team to work on
  + Sprint
    - A time-boxed iteration in Scrum where work is done
    - It can be 1 to 4 weeks
  + Daily scrum
    - A meeting of 15 or fewer minutes every day of the sprint
  => Scrum master
  => Product owner

# The founding principles of Scrum
  + characteristics of a team help to move the Scrum downfield. Those are:
    . Built-in-instability
    . Self-organizing teams
    . Overlapping development phases
    . Multi-learning
    . Subtle control
    . Organizational transfer of learning

# Introduction to Kanban, XP, and Lean
  + Kanban
    - Means kan = sign ban = board in Japanese language
  + XP : extreme programming
    - Aims to improve product quality
    - Test first development
    - Activities: Designing, Coding, Testing, Listening
    - Innivatove practices: Pair programming, Continouns integration and refactoring
  + Lean
    - define value
    - Map value stream
    - Create flow
    - Establish pull
    - Pursue perfection
