# A brief history of Agile
  + Waterfall methodology
    - Sequential or linear approach
  + Agile
    - Iterative
  + Agile project management
    - An approach to project and team management that embodies 'agility' and is based on the Agile menifesto.
    - The menifesto is a collection of four values and 12 principles that define the mindset that all Agile 
      teams should strive for.

  + Business collaboration
    - Welcome changing requirements
  + Team dynamics and culture
    - Build projects around motivated individuals

    - Requirements, and designs emerge from self-organizing teams.

    . Working software over comprehensive documentation
    . Customer collaboration over contract negotiation
    . Responding to change over following a plan

  + 12 principles of Agile:
    1. Our highest priority is to satisfy the customer through early and continuous

    3. Deliver working software frequently, from a couple of weeks to a couple of months, 
      with a preference to the shorter timescale.
    4. Business people and developers must work together daily throughout the project.
    5. Deliver working software frequently, from a couple of weeks to a couple of months,

    6. Build projects around motivated individuals. Give them the environment and support

    7. Working software is the primary measure of progress.

# Adopting an Agile mindset
  + VUCA
    - An acronym that defines the conditions that effect organizations in a changing and comples world.
    => Designed by american army 
    > Volatility
      - Rate of change and churn in a bussiness or situation.
    > Uncertainty
      - Lack of predictability of high potential for surprise
    > Complexity
      - The high number of interrelated forces, issues, organizations, and factors that
        would influence the project.
    > Ambiguity
      - Possibility of misunderstanding the conditions and root causes of events 
        or circumstances.
