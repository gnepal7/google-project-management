# Completing a closeout report
  - Also known as good project heygine 
  + A project closeout report includes:
    . Project summary
    . Methodology
    . Performance baseline
    . Outcomes
    . Lesson learned
    . Next steps
    . Project documentation archive
  > An effective closeout report:
    . Helps ensure that everyone is satisfied with the work that was done
    . Finalizes the efforts of the team and lets people move on to new 
      projects and tasks
    . Increases the impact of the team's work through comunication with other 
      people who may not have been as involved in the project

# Project impact report
  + Reporting your impact can help you to:
    . Analyse results to adapt and improve serviess
    . Motivate staff and senior stakeholders through celebrating achievements
    . Build trust and credibility with supporters, sponsors, funders, and anyone
      benefiting from the project
    . Share lessons with similar organizations
