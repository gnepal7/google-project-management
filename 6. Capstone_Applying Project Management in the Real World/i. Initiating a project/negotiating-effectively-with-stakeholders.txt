# Completing a stakeholder analysis
  + Common project negotiations:
    > Scope
    > Costs and benefits
    > Timeline
    > Success criteria
    > Roles and responsibilities
    > Resources

# Stakeholder management: Tips and takeaways
  + Stakeholder management during project initiation
    . Identify all the stakeholders at the beginning of your project or initiave
    . Keep the project vision clear
    . Equip your stakeholders with user-friendly resources at all times
  + Stakeholder management throughout the project life cycle
    . Find out what stakeholders care about and why
    . Adjust your communication frequency and approach based on stakeholder roles and preferences
    . Enlist the help of senior stakeholders when necessary
    Once stakeholders have a vested, bring project problems to them

# Finding mutually beneficial solutions
  + Enhance communication
  + Clarify wants and needs
  + Achieve workable solutions for everyone involved
  > Mutual benefit
    - When all parties involved gain some kind of benefit or advantage

# Negotiating scope with stakeholders
  + Tips for navigating scope with stakeholders
    - Understand motivations
    - Set the scene
    - Listen first
    - Ask questions to define goals
    - Explain the 'why' before the 'what'
    - Do not oversell
    - Be creative
    - Do not make it personal
    - Seek a win-win outcome (consider on mutually beneficial agreement)
    
# Achieving a win-win outcome
  + Best practices for reaching a mutually beneficial agreement
    - Share information
    - Ask questions and listen actively to responses
    - Propose multiple options whenever possible

# Applying influence in negotiations
  + Conger's steps to effectively influence:
    . Establish credibility
    . Frame for common ground
    . Provide evidence
    . Connect emotionally
  + Coalition
    - A temporary alliance or partnering of individuals or groups in order to achieve a common purpose or to engage in a joint activity
    . Boosts your credibility
    . Find common ground and provide evidence
    . Can connect emotionally
