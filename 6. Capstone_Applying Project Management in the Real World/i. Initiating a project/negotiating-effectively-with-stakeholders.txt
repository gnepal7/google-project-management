# Completing a stakeholder analysis
  + Common project negotiations:
    > Scope
    > Costs and benefits
    > Timeline
    > Success criteria
    > Roles and responsibilities
    > Resources

# Stakeholder management: Tips and takeaways
  + Stakeholder management during project initiation
    . Identify all the stakeholders at the beginning of your project or initiave
    . Keep the project vision clear
    . Equip your stakeholders with user-friendly resources at all times
  + Stakeholder management throughout the project life cycle
    . Find out what stakeholders care about and why
    . Adjust your communication frequency and approach based on stakeholder roles and perrerences
    . Anlist the help of senior stakeholders when necessary
    Once stakeholders have a vested, bring project problems to them
