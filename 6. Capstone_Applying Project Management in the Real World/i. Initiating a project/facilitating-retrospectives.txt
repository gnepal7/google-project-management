# The value of retrospectives
  + Benefits of retrospectives:
    . Encourage team buildings
    . Facilitate improved collaboration
    . Promote positive changes
    
# Retrospectives: Encouraging participation
  + Techniques to encourage participation:
    . Establish a safe environment for the team
    . Model the kind of participation you'd like to elicit from your team
    . Post a group question and ask for individual responses

# Retrospectives: Encouraging accountability
  > Accountability 
    . Encourages the team to think holistically
  + Techniques to encourage accountability:
    . Come prepared with specific challenges to discuss as a group
    . Turn team complaints into SMART action items
      SMART = specific, measurable, attainable, relevant, time-bound
    . Push the team to idenfity its role in creaging a given challenge
    . Detach the challenge being discussed from any specific person in the room
  => Constructive Critism

# Retrospectives: Addressing negativity
  > Psychological safe
  + Techniques for addressing negativity in retrospectives:
    . Aim to set a posivite tone at the start of the meeting
    . Determine how you'll set the tone of the meeting
    . Try anticipating potential negativity by meeting among team members
    . Consider asking team members indivitually to share their thoughts
    . Call a meeting break
