# Time estimation: Three-point estimating
  + Three-point estimating
    - It uses optimistic and pessimistic calculations, meaning calculations based on the best-case and the worst-case scenarios. 
    - Each task receives three estimates:
      . Optimistic
      . Most likely
      . Pessimistic
    - Each estimate indicates the amount of time a task will take under that category

# The three-point estimating technique
   . Optimistic
    > Assumes the best case scenario that issues will not occur
   . Most likely
    > Assumes some issues might occur and based on how long the task usually takes under normal circumstances
   . Pessimistic
    > Assumes the worse case scenario that issues will definitely occur

# Time estimation: Effective time estimate negotiation
