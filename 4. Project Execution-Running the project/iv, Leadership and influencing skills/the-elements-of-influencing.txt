# Steps to effective influencing
  + Influencing 
    - The ability to alter another person's thinking or behaviors
  + According to Jay A. Conger:
    1. Establish credibility
    2. Frame for common ground
    3. Provide evidence
    4. Connect emotionally

  + Some common influencing mistakes
    . Approaching the audience aggressively
    . Resisting compromise
    . Failing to establish credibility, frame for common ground, 
      or connect emotionally
