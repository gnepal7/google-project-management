# Introduction: Leadership and influencing skills

# The necessity of project teamwork
  + Team 
    - A group of people who plan, solve problems, make decisions, 
      and review progress in service of a specific project or objective.
  + Teamwork 
    - An efective, collaborative way of working in which each person is 
      committed to end heading towards a shared goal
    > It fosters creativity
    > Encourages accountability
    > Helps you get stuff done

# The factors that impact team effectiveness
  + 5 Factors
    > Psychological safety
      - An individual's perception of the consequences of taking
        an interpersonal risk
    > Dependability
      - Team members are raliable and complete their work on time
    > Structure and clarity
      - An individual's understanding of job expectations, knowledge of how to meet those
        expectations, and the consequences ot their performance
    > Meaning
      - Finding a sense of purpose either in the work itself or in the results of that work
    > Impact
      - The beliefe that the results belief to change
