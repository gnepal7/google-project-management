# The purpose of a retrospective
  + Retrospective 
    - A workshop or meeting that gives project teams time to reflect on a project
  + Three main purposes of retrospectives:
    . Encourage team building
    . Facilitate improved collaboration
    . Promote positive changes
  + Reasons to hold a retrospective:
    . Missed deadlines of expectations
    . Miscommunications between stakeholders
    . Reached end of a sprint
    . Project launches and landings
    . Record key lessons that other people can learn from
  + Retrospective best practices
    => Changing perepective 'you' to 'we'
    > Ensure discussion is blameless
    > Reflect on positive aspects of the project as well as the negatives
    
# Conducting a retrospective
  > Maintain a positive tone throughout the retrospective
  > The retrospective should be considered a positive experience, where team 
    members feel comfortable sharing their feedback.
  > be consider about team outside of your own.
