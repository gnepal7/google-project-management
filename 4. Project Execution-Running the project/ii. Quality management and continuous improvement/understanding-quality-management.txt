# Introduction: Quality management and continuous improvement

# Key quality management concepts
  + Quality
    - When you fulfill the outlined requirements for the deliverable and meet or exceed the needs
      or expectations of your customers.
  + Four concepts or quality management
    => Quality standards
      - Provide requirements, specifications, or guidelines that can be used to ensure that products,
        processes, or services are fit for achieving the desired outcome.
      > Reliability standards
      > Usability standards
      > Product standards
    => Quality planning
      - The actions of the project manager ir the team to establish a process for identifying and 
        determining exactly which standards of quality are relevant to the project as a whole.
      > Reliability
    => Quality assurance
      - Evaluating if your project is moving towards delivering a high-quality service or product.
    => Quality control
      - Involves monitoring project results and delivery to determine if they are meeting desired
        results or not.
