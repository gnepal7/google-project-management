# Continuous improvement and process improvement
  + Process improvement
    - The practice of identifying, analyzing, and improving existing process to enhance the performance 
      of your team and develop the bese practices, or to optimize consumer experiences.

# Data-driven improvement frameworks
  + Data-driven improvement frameworks
    - Techniques used to make decisions based on actual data
  > DMAIC
    . Define
    . Measure
    . Analyze
    . Improve 
    . Control 
  > PDCA
    - A four-step process that focuses on identifying a problem, fixing that issue, assessing whether
      the fix was successful, and fine-tuning the final fix.
    . Plan 
    . Do : fix the problem
    . Check : about progresses
    . Act : fine tune the fix 
  > These helps to:
    . Identify issues
    . Reduce errors
    . Optimize your processes
