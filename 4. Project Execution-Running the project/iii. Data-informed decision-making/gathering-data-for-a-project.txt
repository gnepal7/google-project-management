# Introduction: Data-informed decision-making

# The value of data
  + Data
    - Data is a collection of facts or information
  + Benefits of using data  
    . Make better decisions
    . Solve problems
    . Understand performance
    . Improve processes
    . Understand your users

# Common types of project data
  + Metric
    - A quantifiable measurement that is used to track and asses a business objective
  + Types of metrics:
    > Productivity metric
      - Metrics that allow you to track effectiveness and efficiency of your project
      . Miliestones
      . Tasks
      . Projections
      . Duration
    > Quality metric
      - Metric that relate to achieving acceptable outcomes      
      . Number of changes
      . Issues
      . Cost variance
  + Tools
    > Project management : Workfend, jira
    > Data management : tableau
