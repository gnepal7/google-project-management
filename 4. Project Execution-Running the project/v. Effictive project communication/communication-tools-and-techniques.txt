# Principles of effective email writing
  + Principles of effective email writing are:
    . State what you want clearly.
    . Keep the content short and concise.
    . Structure your writing.
    . Check grammar, punctuation, and spelling.
  + Structure your writing
    . Use bullets
    . Use labels
    . Add hyperlinks
    . Write a strong topic sentence
  + Best practices for using AI
    . Always check your organization's AI policy
    . Leverage the prompting framework to generate better emails
      > Specify the task
      > Provide necessary context
      > Include references as examples
      > Evaluate your output
      > Iterate for better results
    . Utilize AI to generate templates
    . Customize for your audience
    . Use AI responsibly
    . Always review AI outputs for accuracy
