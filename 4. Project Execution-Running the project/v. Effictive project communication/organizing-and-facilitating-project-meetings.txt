# How to organize effective meetings
  + Effective meeting
    1. Structured
      . Start and end on time
      . Carefully selected attendees
      . Prioritized topics
      . Designated note-taker
    2. Time boxing
      . Setting a time limit
    3. Intentional
      . Clearly stated plan
    4. Collaboration 
      . Active listening, respect each-other
    5. Inclusivity 
      - The practice of policy of including people who might otherwise be excluded or marginalized. 

# Facilitating inclusive and accessible meetings
  + Creating an inclusive environment
    1. Formalize initial check-ins for the group that build understanding and ensure everyone knows their input is needed.
    2. Give everyone your full attention
    3. Help all participants to be heard
    4. Help participants feel comfortable sharing different perspectives
    5. Use images that reflect the diversity of the world
  + Making meetings accessible to everyone
    - When planning your meetings, you should consider the needs of people experiencing the following types of disabilities:
    . Visual impairments and blindness
    . Hearing loss and deafness
    . Mobility disabilities
    . Neurological disorders
