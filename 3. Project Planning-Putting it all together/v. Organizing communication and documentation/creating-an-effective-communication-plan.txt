# Tips for effective communication

# Starting a communication plan
  + Communication plan  
    - Organizes and documents the process, types, and espectations of 
      communication for the project
  + We have to consider the following points, when communicate:
    1. What needs to be communicated?
    2. Who needs to communicate?
    3. When communication needs to happen?
    4. Why and how to communicate?
    5. Where the information being communicated is stored?

# Best practices for building a communication plan
  + Tips for creating your communication plan 
    => Identify, identify, identify
      Before you begin creating the plan, answer these questions to 
      ensure that you have all of the relevant information:
      . Project stakeholders
      . Communication frequently and method
      . Goals
      . Barriers
    => Document and develop
      . Add a column for notes
      . Use formatting to highlight any key details in the plan
      . Ensure that the team can access your document
      . Test your plan
