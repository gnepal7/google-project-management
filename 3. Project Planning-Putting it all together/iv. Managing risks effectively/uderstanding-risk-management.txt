# Introduction: Managing risks effectively

# The importance of risk management
  + Risk
    - A potential event which can occur and can impact your project
  + Issue
    - A known or real problem that can affect the ability to complete a task
  + Risk management
    - The process of identifying and evaluating potential risks and issues that could impact a project
  + Risk management provides an understanding of 
    > What could go wrong
    > Who you'll need to consult
    > How the risk could be mitigated

# Stanton: Managing my first project

# Phases of risk management
  + Steps of risk management:
    1. Identify the risk.
    2. Analyze the risk.
    3. Evaluate the risk.
    4. Treat the risk. 
    5. Monitor and control the risk.

# Uncover opportunities using risk management
  + Some opportunities can be uncovered using risk management:
    . Completing a milestone ahead of schedule
    . Discounted materials 
    . Availability of additional resources (people, investments, equipment)
